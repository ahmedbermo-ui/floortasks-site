Floor Tasks

A simple task list for small teams: shops, cafés, restaurants, cleaning crews, warehouses, workshops, and anyone who hands out work every day.

  • Write the task and who it is for, in seconds.
  • Send it to them on WhatsApp or SMS with one tap.
  • Mark it finished with a short note on how it went.
  • No sign-up, no account, works offline. English and Arabic.
